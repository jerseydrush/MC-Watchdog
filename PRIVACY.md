# Privacy Policy — MC Watchdog

**Effective Date:** November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **MC Watchdog** ("the Bot") collects, uses, and protects your information when you interact with the Bot on Discord or Telegram.

## 1. Information We Collect
MC Watchdog collects and stores only the minimum data required to function:
* **Platform User Identifier:** Your unique numeric Discord User ID or Telegram User ID (stored with a platform prefix, such as `dc_` or `tg_`).
* **User Preferences:** Your configured bot settings, including your selected UTC timezone offset, notification preferences (hourly updates and status alerts), and display toggles (showing owner, moderators, or player lists).

We do **not** collect your messages, passwords, IP addresses, email addresses, or any personal profile information beyond your User ID.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
The collected data is used exclusively to:
* Deliver automated Minecraft server status updates and alerts that you have opted into.
* Format timestamps according to your chosen UTC timezone offset.
* Customize the layout of status messages based on your display preferences.

## 3. Data Storage and Security
Your User ID and preferences are stored locally in restricted configuration files on the host server running the Bot. We take reasonable technical precautions to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure of this data.

## 4. Data Sharing and Third Parties
We do **not** sell, trade, rent, or share your data with any third parties, advertisers, or external analytics services.

## 5. Data Retention and Your Rights
* **Opting Out:** You can disable all automated notifications at any time by using the `/stop` command or adjusting your toggles in `/settings`.
* **Data Deletion:** Your preferences remain stored until you request their removal. If you wish to have your User ID and all associated settings permanently deleted from our storage, please contact the Bot administrator via Discord or Telegram.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in the Bot's functionality. Any updates will be reflected by the "Effective Date" at the top of this document.
